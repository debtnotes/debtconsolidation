# Secured debt

Secured debt is backed by collateral. A mortgage, HELOC, or friend’s recorded deed of trust can make the home collateral. If the borrower defaults, the creditor may pursue foreclosure remedies under applicable law.

Most credit-card debt is unsecured. Replacing it with a home-secured loan may lower an interest rate but can increase the risk of losing housing. Return to [friend assistance](../friends/) or [options](../options/).
