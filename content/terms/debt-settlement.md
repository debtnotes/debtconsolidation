# Debt settlement

Debt settlement means attempting to persuade a creditor to accept less than the full balance. It is distinct from a DMP and debt consolidation. Many programs have the person stop paying cards and accumulate money for settlements, which can increase interest, fees, credit damage, collection pressure, and lawsuit risk before any settlement is reached.

Evaluate total service fees, taxes on canceled debt, and a realistic monthly set-aside before enrolling. A company cannot guarantee a result. Return to [scam screening](../scams/) or [options](../options/).
