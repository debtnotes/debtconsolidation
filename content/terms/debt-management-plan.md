# Debt management plan (DMP)

A DMP is a structured repayment plan commonly offered through a nonprofit credit-counseling organization. It may consolidate payments administratively and seek lower rates or waived fees, but it commonly requires full repayment and a regular monthly payment. Initial counseling may be free while the plan has fees.

The central test is not whether the organization will enroll the person; it is whether the payment remains affordable after housing, food, medicine, utilities, taxes, insurance, and a safety buffer. Return to [options](../options/) or [scam screening](../scams/).
