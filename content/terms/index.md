# Key-term guides

Use these short pages when a term affects a real decision:

- [SSDI](./ssdi/) — disability insurance and protected benefit payments.
- [SSI](./ssi/) — needs-based benefits, gifts, loans, and resource limits.
- [Debt management plan](./debt-management-plan/) — structured repayment through credit counseling.
- [Debt settlement](./debt-settlement/) — paying less than the full balance and its risks.
- [Judgment lien](./judgment-lien/) — why a court judgment can matter to a homeowner.
- [Secured debt](./secured-debt/) — why a loan against the home changes the stakes.
