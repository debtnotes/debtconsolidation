# SSDI — Social Security Disability Insurance

SSDI is a disability-insurance benefit generally based on a worker’s earnings record. It is not SSI and does not use SSI’s ordinary $2,000/$3,000 resource limit. That does not mean a large transaction is harmless: other means-tested programs, taxes, bank-account handling, budgeting, and bankruptcy disclosure can still matter.

For ordinary private card debt, SSDI has strong federal anti-garnishment protection. Keep direct deposits and records clear, but do not assume the protection resolves a judgment lien against a home. Return to [benefits](../benefits/) or [FAQ](../faq/).
