# SSI — Supplemental Security Income

SSI is a federal needs-based benefit. It generally has a $2,000 resource limit for one person and $3,000 for a couple. A cash gift, funds kept in an account, loan proceeds, a house sale, or a property transfer can change eligibility or reporting obligations.

A genuine enforceable loan is generally not SSI income, but cash held into the following month can still be a countable resource. Verify the benefit before accepting any friend help. Return to [benefits](../benefits/) or [FAQ](../faq/).
