# Maryland help and referral list

Availability, county coverage, income eligibility, conflicts, and fees can change. Confirm the current scope at intake. No listing is an endorsement of a particular lawyer.

| Resource | Service | Verified access |
|---|---|---|
| [Maryland Legal Aid](https://www.mdlab.org/) | Civil legal-aid screening/referral; consumer debt, benefits, housing, or bankruptcy-related scope varies by office/capacity. | Statewide organization; confirm county/service at intake. |
| [Maryland Volunteer Lawyers Service](https://mvlslaw.org/free-legal-help/eligibility/) | Legal screening/referral; consumer clinic and Bankruptcy Bypass pathway. | Online intake; phone Mon–Thu, 9 a.m.–noon: 410-547-6537 or 800-510-0050. |
| [MVLS consumer / Bankruptcy Bypass](https://mvlslaw.org/volunteer/consumer-law-resources/) | Bankruptcy screening and potential limited-scope/pro bono pathway. | Use MVLS intake; home-equity cases need individualized screening. |
| [U.S. Bankruptcy Court Debtor Assistance Project](https://www.mdb.uscourts.gov/programs-services/debtor-assistance-project) | Free virtual half-hour consultation with volunteer bankruptcy lawyer; no filing/representation. | Complete online intake then schedule; MVLS intake can assist during its hours. |
| [Court low-bono Chapter 7 list](https://www.mdb.uscourts.gov/files/2022%20Low%20Bono%20Ch.%207%20Listing.pdf) | Referral to lawyers who may offer reduced fee; not an endorsement. | Confirm current roster with DAP/MVLS because linked list is dated. |
| [Maryland Court Help Center](https://www.courts.state.md.us/helpcenter) | Free brief legal/court-form help in civil cases; not full representation. | 410-260-1392, 8:30 a.m.–8 p.m.; chat/in-person options vary. |
| [Maryland Senior Legal Helpline](https://aging.maryland.gov/Pages/legal-services.aspx) | Legal advice/referral for Marylanders 60+; verify consumer/bankruptcy scope. | 410-396-1322 or 800-896-4213; verify hours. |
| [Disability Rights Maryland](https://disabilityrightsmd.org/) | Disability-rights/benefits advocacy and referral. | Use current intake/contact details on site; scope/capacity varies. |
| [Maryland Access Point](https://marylandaccesspoint.211md.org/) | Benefits counseling/referral and aging/disability supports. | 1-844-627-5465; local MAP/211 connection. |
| [CASH Campaign of Maryland](https://cashmd.org/) | Financial coaching/planning and tax/financial-capability resources. | Confirm current appointment/location availability. |
| [National Foundation for Credit Counseling](https://www.nfcc.org/) | Nonprofit credit counseling/DMP referral network. | Ask about fees, included cards, missed-payment consequences, and total payoff. |
| [Money Management International](https://www.moneymanagement.org/) | NFCC-member nonprofit credit counseling/DMP that serves Maryland remotely. | Confirm current Maryland availability and plan fees/terms. |
| [HUD housing-counselor search](https://www.hud.gov/housingcounseling) | HUD-approved housing counseling, including reverse-mortgage/refinance/foreclosure questions. | Search by ZIP/county and confirm approval/cost. |
| [Maryland Office of Financial Regulation](https://labor.maryland.gov/finance/consumers.shtml) | Consumer-finance information, complaints, and licensing resources. | Use for lender/servicer concerns; it is not personal representation. |
| [FTC ReportFraud](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/) | Report suspected debt-relief, mortgage-relief, recovery, and impersonation scams. | Preserve the solicitation, contract, payment records, and contact details first. |

## Match the resource to the need

- **A lawsuit, judgment, lien, or bank freeze:** Maryland Court Help + consumer/bankruptcy screening.
- **Home equity or a bankruptcy question:** DAP/MVLS/low-bono consultation before any lien or title move.
- **SSI, Medicaid, SNAP, or housing assistance:** MAP plus the program administrator/benefits professional before receiving funds or changing title.
- **Repayment-plan comparison:** NFCC member or MMI, while also asking a lawyer about the home/judgment facts.
- **An ad, call, or text promises fast relief:** read the [scam-screening guide](./scams/) before sharing information or paying anyone.
- **Reverse mortgage, refinance, foreclosure, or sale:** HUD counselor plus independent attorney/benefits review.

When the county is known, ask MAP, Court Help, MVLS, and Maryland Legal Aid for county-specific organizations. Do not assume statewide coverage for every type of case.
