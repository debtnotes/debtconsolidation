Key-term guides/Judgment lien

Judgment lien

A judgment lien is a lien arising from a court judgment that may attach to real property when properly recorded. It can complicate a sale, refinance, inheritance, or estate administration. It does not necessarily mean an immediate forced home sale, but it makes home value, existing mortgages, ownership, and exemptions important.

Do not ignore a summons or judgment notice. Return to creditors and the home or FAQ.
